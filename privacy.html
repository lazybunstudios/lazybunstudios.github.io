Privacy Policy

Our applications are designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect or store any personal information from users. Any data that is collected, such as game save data for the purpose of local leaderboards, remains entirely on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers or any third-party servers. You have full control over this data. If you wish to remove it, you can do so by simply uninstalling the application from your device.

Data Collected

The application only stores essential game-related data (such as game save data or local leaderboard scores) locally on your device. This data:

  • is not personal data,
  • is not transmitted to us or to any third parties,
  • and we do not use analytics, advertising, tracking tools, or online services.

Legal Basis

Since we do not collect or process personal data, no GDPR legal basis for data processing is required.

Data Storage & Removal

All data remains on your device. You may delete all locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the application.

User Rights

Although we do not process personal data, users may still contact us regarding any privacy-related questions. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this privacy policy will be provided through future application updates on our official website.
